# Chess Royale

## Overview
Chess Royale is a competitive chess game inspired by Clash Royale. It blends classic chess rules with modern live-service features: ranked matchmaking, seasonal progression, cosmetics, gacha-style chests, and guild wars.

## Core Modes
### 1) Solo vs Computer
- Multiple AI difficulty tiers (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Master).
- Skill ratings per tier to track progress and unlock rewards.

### 2) Ranked vs Friends
- Invite friends to rated matches.
- Elo-based rating adjustments with safeguards against win-trading.

### 3) Ranked Matchmaking (Random Players)
- Match players by rating and region.
- Queue estimate, role protection (prevent repeat opponents), and ping-based filters.

## Rating & Matchmaking
- Elo/MMR hybrid for stable progression.
- Seasonal soft reset and placement matches.
- Anti-smurf and anti-boosting detection (suspicious streaks, repeated opponent checks).

## Economy & Rewards
### Coins
- Earned from ranked wins, daily missions, and events.
- Used to buy cosmetic skins for pieces and boards.

### Chests (Gacha-Style)
- Chest tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Royal).
- Drop tables with cosmetics, coin bundles, and emotes.
- Pity system to guarantee rare drops over time.

## Progression Systems
### Battle Pass
- Free and Premium tracks.
- XP earned from playing matches, missions, and guild activities.
- Season-exclusive cosmetics and chessboard themes.

### Levels & Milestones
- Account levels grant cosmetics, titles, and profile frames.
- Ranked milestones unlock special badges and borders.

## Guilds & Guild Wars
- Guild creation with shared chat and weekly goals.
- Guild Wars: team-based ranked ladder with match quotas.
- Rewards: guild chests, unique skins, and event tickets.

## Cosmetics
- Skins for all pieces, boards, and UI themes.
- Emotes and victory animations.
- Limited-time seasonal sets.

## Competitive Integrity
- Fair-play monitoring with cheat detection.
- Move validation and server-side game logic.
- Report system and matchmaking bans for violations.

## Live Ops & Events
- Seasonal tournaments.
- Time-limited challenges with unique rewards.
- Collaboration-themed cosmetic drops.

## Monetization (Cosmetic-Only)
- Premium battle pass.
- Cosmetic bundles and event chests.
- No pay-to-win gameplay advantages.

## Technical Scope (MVP)
- Chess engine integration (e.g., Stockfish for AI).
- Real-time matchmaking and game server.
- Player profiles, inventory, and progression services.
- Secure economy and reward distribution.

## Roadmap (High-Level)
1. **MVP**: Core chess, matchmaking, ratings, AI tiers.
2. **Phase 2**: Cosmetics, inventory, chest rewards.
3. **Phase 3**: Battle pass, guilds, guild wars.
4. **Phase 4**: Events, tournaments, collaboration drops.
